Probability & Random Variables

Random variable: a numerical function that assigns a real number to every outcome of a random experiment. A discrete random variable takes countable values; a continuous random variable takes values over intervals.

PMF: for a discrete random variable, p(x)=P(X=x), with p(x)≥0 and Σp(x)=1. PDF: for a continuous random variable, probabilities are obtained from areas under the density curve.

Expectation: for discrete X, E[X]=Σxp(x). For continuous X with density f(x), E[X]=∫xf(x)dx over the support.

Variance: Var(X)=E[X²]−[E(X)]². Standard deviation is σ=√Var(X).
