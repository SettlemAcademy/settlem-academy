Privacy Policy

Settlem Academy respects your privacy. This page explains, at a high level, how information may be used when you use this website.

When you register, contact us or use learning features, information such as your name, email address, phone number, course selections and learning activity may be processed to provide the requested service.

How information is used

  • To respond to enquiries and provide support.

Third-party services

Some features may use third-party services such as YouTube or hosting/payment providers. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

Data security

We use reasonable technical measures for account and service protection. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
